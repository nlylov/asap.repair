A faucet swap is one of the most predictable handyman jobs — when the sink, hole count, shut-offs and supply lines match the new faucet. That is why we review photos first: a corroded shut-off or a three-hole sink for a single-hole faucet changes the job. Prices below are labor; the faucet, supply lines and any adapter are yours or quoted separately.

The ranges are the same ones the faucet page calculator quotes.

What drives the price

  • Faucet type: single-hole, centerset, widespread and wall-mount all mount differently.
  • Shut-off valves: a valve that will not close has to be replaced before the faucet comes off.
  • Access: pedestal sinks and tight vanities take longer than an open kitchen base.
  • Sink swap: a new sink with the faucet is its own line — see the second table.
  • Building rules: COI paperwork and water shut-off notice in co-ops and condos.

What's included

  • Removing the old faucet and cleaning the deck
  • Connecting compatible supply lines and the new faucet
  • Leak test under pressure before we leave
  • Sprayer, soap dispenser or drain assembly when they come with the faucet

What's quoted separately

  • The faucet, supply lines, adapters and drain assembly — the parts themselves
  • Shut-off valve replacement and new supply lines are included in the tiers that name them; on a straight swap they are quoted separately
  • Sink replacement, countertop cutting or cabinet modification — see the sink table

When it's not a handyman job

  • Anything that alters the piping behind the wall or under the floor is a Licensed Master Plumber's work — we identify the boundary from photos before scheduling.

Frequently asked questions

Can you replace a faucet if the shut-off valves are stuck?

Often yes, by replacing the valve as a separate line. If the building main has to be shut, we coordinate that with your super before the visit.

Do you install the faucet I bought?

Yes. Send the model link and a photo of the sink from above and below so we can confirm hole count and fit.

What if the sink needs to be replaced too?

Sink installation is its own line in the second table; drop-in and undermount are priced differently.

How long does a faucet swap take?

Most kitchen and bathroom swaps are done in a single visit.
