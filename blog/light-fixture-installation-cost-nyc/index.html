What drives the price

  • Fixture weight: heavy fixtures may require additional support before hanging.
  • Ceiling height: double-height and stairwell fixtures need taller ladders or scaffolding.
  • Canopy size: a new canopy that does not cover the old opening means patching.
  • Dimmers: LED fixtures and old dimmers do not always agree — we check compatibility.
  • Assembly: crystal and multi-arm chandeliers can take longer to assemble than to hang.

What's included

  • Taking down the old fixture and checking the box
  • Hanging and wiring the new fixture to the existing connection
  • Dimmer compatibility review
  • Function test before we leave

What's quoted separately

  • The fixture, bulbs and dimmer
  • Additional support or a new box for heavy fixtures
  • Patching and painting around a smaller canopy

When it's not a handyman job

  • New fixture locations may require a new box, electrical routing, a NYC Licensed Master Electrician and a DOB permit. We replace fixtures where they are; we do not add locations.

Frequently asked questions

Can you hang a heavy chandelier?

Yes, after a weight and support review from photos. Heavy fixtures may need additional support, which is quoted first.

Can you move a light to a new spot on the ceiling?

No — a new location means new wiring, which is Licensed Master Electrician and DOB permit territory in NYC.

Do you install dimmers?

Replacing a compatible switch with a dimmer is a switch installation, quoted as its own line.

What should I send for a quote?

The fixture link or weight, ceiling height, a photo of the current fixture and any ladder or building access limits.
