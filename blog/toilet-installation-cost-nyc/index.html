How Much Does Toilet Installation Cost in NYC? (2026 Prices)

Running, rocking, or leaking at the base? Most toilet swaps are done in one visit. Here is what a standard, comfort-height, one-piece, wall-hung or smart toilet costs to install in New York City.

Toilet replacement is priced by the model and by what the floor looks like once the old toilet is off. Standard round, elongated, comfort-height, dual-flush and skirted models are all on the list; smart toilets and bidet seats need an existing outlet in reach, so a photo of the wall behind the bowl matters. Prices below are labor; the toilet, wax ring or seal, bolts and supply line are yours or quoted separately.

What drives the price

  • Model: skirted, one-piece and wall-hung toilets take longer to set and align than a standard two-piece.
  • Flange and floor: a cracked flange or soft floor has to be addressed before the new toilet is set.
  • Shut-off valve: a valve that does not close fully gets replaced first.
  • Old toilet disposal: haul-away is quoted separately and depends on building rules.
  • Smart toilets: need a grounded outlet within reach and sometimes a water filter line.

What's included

  • Removing the old toilet and cleaning the flange
  • Setting the new toilet with a fresh seal and bolts
  • Connecting the supply line and testing for leaks
  • Seat installation and flush test

What's quoted separately

  • The toilet, seal, bolts and supply line — the parts themselves
  • Shut-off valve replacement and old-toilet haul-away are included in the tiers that name them; on a straight swap they are quoted separately
  • Bidet seat or smart toilet electrical accessories

When it's not a handyman job

  • If photos show a cracked flange, a soft floor, a hidden leak, a stack issue or anything that means altering pipe, we say so before booking and point you to a Licensed Master Plumber.

Frequently asked questions

Can you install a smart toilet or bidet seat?

Yes, when there is a grounded outlet in reach. Send a photo of the wall behind the bowl and the model link.

Do you take the old toilet away?

Haul-away is quoted separately; some buildings require the super to handle bulk disposal.

What photos speed up the quote?

The existing toilet, the shut-off valve, the floor around the base and a link to the new model.

Is a wall-hung toilet a handyman job?

Replacing one on an existing carrier is. Installing a new in-wall carrier alters the plumbing and is a Licensed Master Plumber's job.
