Best Handyman Services in Queens, NYC: What to Expect & How to Choose

Queens is NYC's most diverse borough — from 1920s attached homes in Forest Hills to modern high-rises in Long Island City. Here's what you need to know about finding a reliable handyman in Queens.

What Handyman Services Are Most In Demand in Queens?

  • Furniture assembly — IKEA deliveries are everywhere in Queens
  • TV and shelf mounting — older plaster walls make this tricky to DIY
  • Minor home repairs — patching holes, fixing doors, replacing hardware
  • AC installation — window units are standard in most Queens apartments
  • Minor plumbing — faucet replacement, toilet repairs, shut-off valves
  • Interior painting — single rooms to full apartments

5 Things to Check Before Hiring a Handyman in Queens

  • Are they insured and COI-ready? In NYC, contractors should carry general liability insurance. Always ask for proof and confirm building COI needs.
  • Can they provide a COI? Co-ops and condos often require a Certificate of Insurance. Not all handymen carry one.
  • Do they review scope before pricing? Hourly pricing can balloon quickly, but fixed promises before photos can also miss access, material, or building-rule issues. Scope-reviewed quotes keep expectations clear.
  • Check Google and Yelp reviews. Look for consistent patterns, especially how they handle problems.
  • Are they local? A Queens-based handyman knows the buildings and logistics. Travel time affects cost.
