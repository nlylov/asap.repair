Common Questions

Appliance Repair FAQ

Do you repair appliances in NYC?

We help with appliance symptoms that fit handyman-compatible diagnosis, cleaning, hookup, drain, vent, leveling, replacement or setup scope after photos, model details, access and building rules are reviewed.

What appliance repair issues are a good fit?

Good-fit requests include refrigerator temperature checks and cleaning, dishwasher leaks or drain problems, washer vibration or drain issues, dryer vent or heat checks, loose appliance connections, and deciding whether repair or replacement makes more sense.

What appliance repair work is outside scope?

Sealed-system refrigerator work, refrigerant, compressor replacement, board-level electronics, new gas piping, hidden plumbing, new electrical circuits, hardwired changes, warranty-authorized service and permit-level work may require the manufacturer, a licensed trade or a specialized appliance technician.

Can you install a replacement appliance?

Yes. We can quote compatible replacement installation for dishwashers, washers, dryers, refrigerators, microwaves and ranges when existing hookups, shutoffs, venting, power, access and building rules fit.

What should I send before booking?

Send the appliance brand and model number, photos of the appliance and hookups, the exact symptom, any error code, when the issue happens, water, drain, vent, gas or power photos when relevant, building access details, and whether the appliance is under warranty.
