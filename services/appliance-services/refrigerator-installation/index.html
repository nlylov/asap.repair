Common Questions

Refrigerator FAQ

How much does installation cost?

Pricing depends on scope, access, size, door reversal, leveling, old-unit move-out, and whether an existing compatible water line and shutoff are already present.

Can you connect the ice maker water line?

We can connect to an existing compatible refrigerator water line and shutoff after photo review. New plumbing lines, hidden piping, saddle-valve corrections, or permit-level scope may require a Licensed Master Plumber.

Do you reverse refrigerator doors?

Yes, when the model supports door reversal and the required hardware, clearance, and hinge access are available.

Can you remove the old fridge?

We can disconnect and move the old unit when the route, elevator, flooring protection, and building rules are reviewed. Hauling can be arranged after scope review.

What should I send for a refrigerator installation quote?

Send the refrigerator model link, delivery status, kitchen and access-path photos, width and height clearances, water-line and shutoff photos if ice maker hookup is needed, door reversal needs, old-unit move-out needs, and building requirements.
