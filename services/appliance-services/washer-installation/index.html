Need a washer installed in New York City? Repair Asap LLC installs compatible washing machines across Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, and Staten Island. We review top-load, front-load, stackable, compact apartment washer, and laundry closet setups around the model, hookups, floor level, and building access.

Every washer installation includes supply-line hookup, drain connection, leveling, leak testing, and a full cycle test. We check hose fit, shutoff valves, drain height, and vibration before leaving the unit ready to use.

We quote appliance installation across Queens, Manhattan, Brooklyn, the Bronx, Staten Island, and Western Long Island or Nassau County when scope and travel fit. For the fastest estimate, text the appliance model number, a photo of the installation space, existing hookups, and any building or COI requirements.

Washer installation is best scheduled when the hookups are already present but the machine needs proper hose connection, leveling, drain placement, and a leak test before use.

We check the shut-off valves, hose condition, drain hose fit, floor level, vibration clearance, and whether the washer door or lid has enough room to operate after the unit is pushed into place.

If the valves are seized, the drain is too low, or the appliance closet is too tight, we explain the options before forcing the unit into a setup that may leak or shake during the spin cycle.

Common Questions

Washer Installation FAQ

How much does washer installation cost in NYC?

Standard washer installation often runs $120-$200 when existing valves, drain, access, and model fit. Stackable or tight-closet setups are quoted after model and space review.

Do you connect water supply lines?

Yes, when the existing valves and drain are accessible and in usable condition. If valves are seized or the drain setup is not compatible, we flag that before forcing the connection.

Can you install a stackable washer?

Yes, when the washer, dryer, stacking kit, closet clearance, floor level, and building rules are compatible. Send model numbers and closet photos before booking.

Do you remove the old washer?

We can disconnect and move your old unit. Haul-away can be arranged for an additional fee.
