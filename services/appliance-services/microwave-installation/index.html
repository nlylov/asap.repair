Common Questions

Microwave FAQ

How much does installation cost?

Pricing depends on type. OTR units require compatible mounting hardware, cabinet/wall support, vent path, and outlet review; built-in kits are usually simpler. We confirm the quote after scope review.

Do you remove the old microwave?

Yes. We safely disconnect, unmount, and remove the existing unit.

Can you install over-the-range where there wasn't one?

Often, if the cabinet, wall, vent path, and existing outlet location fit the new unit. New outlet, new wiring, or circuit work is reviewed before booking and may require licensed electrical scope.

What should I send for a fast quote?

Send the microwave model number, delivery status, photos of the cabinet, wall, old microwave or empty opening, bracket and template, outlet location, vent direction, trim kit if applicable, and any building or COI requirements.

How long does it take?

OTR: 1–2 hours including removal. Built-in and drawer: about 1 hour.
