Your new appliance is on the truck and the old one is still sitting in the kitchen. Appliance installation in NYC goes smoothest when that handoff is planned: we uncrate the new unit, disconnect the old one and move it to your building's designated spot, connect to the existing water, drain, vent and power hookups where they are compatible, level it, test it and clean up. Full haul-away to a disposal facility is available for an added fee if you would rather not leave it by the curb. Text the model number or product link plus photos of the space and the existing hookups, and the estimate costs you nothing.

When something stops working, the real question is whether it is worth fixing. We look at the symptom, the model, the age and what parts and labor would run, then tell you plainly whether the repair makes sense or whether that money is better spent on a new unit. Photo-reviewed appliance repair covers refrigerator cooling and ice buildup, dishwasher leaks and drain trouble, washer vibration and drain issues, dryer heat and vent problems, and burner, door or leveling faults on ranges. Send the exact symptom and any error code on the display.

NYC buildings decide half the job. A Forest Hills co-op wants a COI before anyone touches a hookup, a Midtown high-rise gives you a freight elevator window, and a fourth-floor walk-up in Brooklyn changes how a refrigerator gets upstairs. We are insured, send COI paperwork to management ahead of the visit, and check clearances, doorways and the access path from your photos so the unit fits where it needs to go.

Gas is where we stay honest with you. We set a gas-ready dryer or range when the existing shut-off, connector and appliance location are already code-ready, and we tell you up front when the job needs a Licensed Master Plumber, gas piping work or a DOB filing instead. New circuits and new plumbing lines work the same way: flagged before scheduling and routed to the right licensed trade, never improvised on site. Commercial cold-side calls follow the same rule, so we triage reach-in coolers, prep tables, beverage coolers, ice machines and walk-ins, then bring in a refrigeration specialist when the fix is sealed-system work.

Photo and text estimates are free. If the job needs eyes on it before it can be priced, the on-site assessment is $99 and that $99 comes off the bill when you hire us; the work itself starts at a $150 minimum, is quoted before anything begins, and NYC sales tax is separate. We are insured, back our installation work with a one-year workmanship warranty, and hold 4.9 stars across 73 verified reviews.
