Need a dishwasher installed in your NYC kitchen? Repair Asap LLC handles swap-outs and scope-reviewed first-time installs for common major brands - Bosch, Miele, Samsung, LG, KitchenAid, Whirlpool, and more. We connect to compatible existing supply, drain, and outlet or hardwire setups across Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, and Staten Island.

Every installation includes old unit removal (if applicable), supply line connection, drain hookup with high loop or air gap, outlet or hardwire compatibility check, and a full leak test. Pricing is confirmed after hookup, cabinet, and building-rule review.

We quote appliance installation across Queens, Manhattan, Brooklyn, the Bronx, Staten Island, and Western Long Island or Nassau County when scope and travel fit. For the fastest estimate, text the appliance model number, a photo of the installation space, existing hookups, and any building or COI requirements.

Related project context: the Queens bathroom build-out shows water shutoff, drain, waterproofing, and finish sequencing, while the Manhattan barbershop renovation shows how access, finished surfaces, and multi-trade coordination are handled around installation work.

This service is a good fit for dishwasher swap-outs, first-time installs in an existing cabinet opening, and kitchens where the drain hose, supply line, or outlet/hardwire connection can be reviewed before the new unit is secured.

On site we check cabinet width, leveling feet, anti-tip brackets, drain routing, water shut-off condition, and whether the outlet or hardwire connection is safe and reachable. The goal is a clean install that does not leak, rock, or leave the door rubbing against adjacent cabinets.

If the old dishwasher left water damage, swollen flooring, or a loose mounting surface, we flag it before installation so the repair can be handled correctly instead of hiding the problem behind the appliance.

Common Questions

Dishwasher FAQ

How much does installation cost?

Pricing is based on hookup complexity. Standard swap-outs with existing compatible hookups are the most affordable. New plumbing, new outlets, or permit-level scope is reviewed before booking.

Can you install one where there wasn't one before?

We review first-time installs from photos before booking. New water supply lines, drain piping, dedicated outlets, new circuits, or permit-level work may require licensed trade scope or DOB permits.

Do you remove the old dishwasher?

Yes. We disconnect, pull out, and can haul away the old unit when the existing setup is safe to disconnect.

How long does installation take?

Standard swap: 1–2 hours. First-time installs or corrections are quoted only after hookup and scope review.
