Common Questions

Range & Oven FAQ

How much does installation cost?

Pricing depends on fuel type, connection access, anti-tip bracket requirements, model fit, building rules, and whether licensed trade scope is needed. We confirm the quote after scope review.

Do you connect gas ranges?

We handle gas range replacement and setup only when the existing shut-off and appliance location are code-ready. If the job requires Licensed Master Plumber scope, DOB filing, gas piping alteration, or correction work, we flag that before scheduling and coordinate the proper licensed-trade next steps.

Do you install the anti-tip bracket?

Yes, when the floor or wall surface, model bracket, and access allow secure installation. Damaged flooring, missing hardware, or building restrictions are reviewed before final scope.

What should I send for a fast quote?

Send the appliance model number, delivery status, photos of the kitchen opening, existing outlet or gas shutoff, cord or connector details, anti-tip bracket hardware, cabinet clearance, and any building or COI requirements.

Can you convert gas to propane?

Only when the manufacturer supplies the correct conversion kit and the scope is legal for the site. If licensed gas work is required, we identify that before scheduling.
