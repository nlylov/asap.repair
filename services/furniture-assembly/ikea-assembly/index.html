NYC Apartment Tips

Assembly in NYC Buildings

IKEA furniture assembly in New York City comes with unique logistics. We handle them all.

Elevator & Freight Access

We coordinate with your doorman, reserve the freight elevator, and handle all building logistics. Just let us know your building's rules when you book.

Walk-Up Buildings

Walk-up buildings are reviewed before booking. Tell us the floor, box count, item weight, and whether boxes are already upstairs because carrying large furniture can change the labor scope.

Certificate of Insurance (COI)

Most co-ops and condos require a COI before any work begins. We provide it for free within 24 hours — just ask when you book.

Wall Anchoring for NYC Walls

Drywall, plaster, brick, concrete, and metal studs all require different anchoring decisions. We review the wall type, item load, manufacturer hardware, and building rules before anchoring PAX wardrobes, BILLY bookcases, and tall dressers.

Common Questions

IKEA Assembly FAQ

How much does IKEA furniture assembly cost in NYC?

Prices depend on the specific item and size. Simple pieces like a KALLAX 1×4 shelf start around $80-$120, while complex systems like a PAX wardrobe with sliding doors can range from $250-$550 after scope review. Use our instant calculator above for a quick estimate, then send product links, box count, and room photos for confirmation.

Do you assemble all IKEA product lines?

We assemble common IKEA series including PAX, KALLAX, MALM, HEMNES, BESTÅ, BILLY, NORDLI, HAVSTA, IDANÄS, and many discontinued lines when instructions, hardware, and room access are available.

Can you anchor IKEA furniture to the wall?

We review IKEA tip-over restraints for tall furniture. Anchoring depends on the included hardware, wall material, stud or anchor access, rental or building rules, and whether the wall can support the item.

What if IKEA shipped missing or damaged parts?

We carry common IKEA hardware (cam locks, dowels, screws) and can often complete the assembly on the spot. For manufacturer-specific parts like drawer rails or hinges, we'll help you request replacements through IKEA before the job can be finished.

How long does IKEA assembly take?

It depends on the item. A KALLAX shelf or MALM nightstand takes about 30–60 minutes. A PAX wardrobe system with interiors typically takes 2–4 hours. Multi-item orders (e.g., bedroom set) may take a full day. We'll give you a time estimate with your quote.

Do you bring your own tools?

Yes. Our technicians bring cordless drills, drivers, levels, stud finders, and common hardware. You should have the furniture, instructions, manufacturer hardware, and clear working space ready.

Can you assemble already-opened or partially built IKEA furniture?

Yes. We can pick up where you left off, or disassemble incorrectly built pieces and start fresh. We've rescued many half-finished PAX and MALM projects.

Do you remove all the packaging?

Packaging breakdown can be included when requested. Removal or haul-away depends on building disposal rules, parking, and the amount of cardboard, styrofoam, and plastic.

Can you assemble IKEA kitchen cabinets?

We can assemble IKEA SEKTION cabinet boxes, doors, drawers, and hardware. Wall mounting, rail fastening, plumbing, electrical, countertop, and full kitchen installation scope must be reviewed separately before booking.

What should I send for an IKEA assembly quote?

Send IKEA product links or article numbers, the PAX planner link if relevant, box count, delivery status, room photos, ceiling height for tall units, wall anchoring needs, packaging or haul-away requests, and any building or COI requirements.

Do you service all NYC boroughs?

Our primary service area covers Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, Staten Island, and Western Long Island or Nassau County when scope and travel fit. For other areas, availability depends on schedule and project scope — contact us to check.
