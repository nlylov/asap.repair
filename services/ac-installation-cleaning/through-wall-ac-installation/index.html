Common Questions

Through-Wall AC FAQ

How much does through-wall AC installation cost in NYC?

Pricing depends on unit size, sleeve condition, trim needs, access, drainage, and whether the existing outlet matches the unit. Send sleeve dimensions, unit label, plug photo, and wall photos before booking.

What is a through-wall AC unit?

A through-wall AC sits in a metal sleeve built into the wall. Unlike window units, they don't block the window and provide a cleaner look. They're common in NYC co-ops and condos.

Can you install a through-wall AC in an existing sleeve?

Yes, when the sleeve, exterior grille, dimensions, drainage, wall condition, and existing outlet are compatible with the replacement unit. New wall openings or facade changes need separate approval and scope review.

Do you handle the electrical for through-wall AC?

We check the existing outlet and unit plug before booking. New outlets, 220V/240V changes, dedicated circuits, or permit-level electrical work must be handled through the proper electrical trade and permit path.

What should I send for a through-wall AC installation quote?

Send sleeve dimensions and photos, unit model or BTU label, plug and outlet photos, wall photos, exterior grille photos if safe, drainage or trim concerns, old-unit removal needs, and any building approval or COI requirements.
