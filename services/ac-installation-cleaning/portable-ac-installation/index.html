Common Questions

Portable AC FAQ

How much does portable AC setup cost in NYC?

Setup typically includes window venting kit fitting, hose routing, drainage configuration, and a basic outlet-location review. Pricing depends on your window type, unit model, panel fit, and access. We provide a quote before starting.

Do you install the window venting kit?

Yes. We fit the exhaust panel to your window or sliding door when the panel and opening are compatible, seal gaps with weatherstripping where practical, and make sure the hose is connected without sharp bends.

Can you set up a portable AC with no window?

In most cases a window or sliding door is needed for venting. However, we can route exhaust or an approved exterior vent path. We do not cut new wall openings or tie a portable AC into a dryer vent without building approval and a scope review. Send photos before booking if the room has no standard window.

Do portable ACs work with casement or slider windows?

Often yes. Casement, slider, and tall narrow openings may need a trimmed panel, adapter kit, or alternate hose route. Send window measurements and photos so we can confirm the setup before the visit.

Do portable ACs need drainage?

Many modern units are self-evaporative, but some require a drain hose or tank emptying. We configure your unit's drainage based on the model, the room layout, and the manufacturer's instructions.

What should I send for a portable AC setup quote?

Send the unit model, window or sliding-door photos, opening measurements, vent kit photos, hose length if known, outlet location, drainage requirements, room layout, and whether an adapter or panel trim may be needed.
