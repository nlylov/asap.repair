For a faster quote, text the AC model or BTU label, window or sleeve photos, measurements, and any building bracket or COI rules.

AC removal is useful at the end of the cooling season, before a move-out, when a window unit is leaking, or when a building requires the unit and bracket to be removed safely.

We remove the unit carefully, protect the window frame and sill, check for loose panels or sharp hardware, and help seal the opening so weather and insects do not come through after removal.

If the unit is heavy, above street level, leaking, or mounted with a bracket, send photos before the visit so we can plan safe handling and avoid damage to the window or facade.

For NYC disposal, air conditioners that contain CFC/Freon normally need a DSNY appointment before collection. Newer R32 or R600a units may need manufacturer or private-carter disposal instead, so send a photo of the warning label if you see one.

Common Questions

AC Removal FAQ

How much does AC removal cost?

Flat-rate pricing is based on unit type, weight, floor level, bracket condition, and whether the unit needs curbside placement, building storage, or disposal coordination. Send photos for the fastest quote.

Do you restore the window after removal?

Yes. We remove the bracket when requested, close and lock the window, check loose panels or hardware, and install weatherstripping or a foam seal when appropriate to reduce drafts.

Can you dispose of the old unit?

We can help remove the unit from the window and coordinate the disposal path. In NYC, residents usually need a DSNY CFC/Freon removal appointment before an air conditioner can be collected. Some newer R32 or R600a units require manufacturer or private-carter disposal instead.

Is fall AC removal required in NYC?

NYC doesn't mandate seasonal removal for most buildings, but many co-ops and condos do. We handle end-of-season removal for tenants, property managers, and building supers.
