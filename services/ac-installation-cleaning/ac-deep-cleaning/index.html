Common Questions

AC Cleaning FAQ

How much does AC deep cleaning cost?

Pricing depends on unit type, access, number of units, and visible condition. Send model photos and inside-condition photos if accessible so we can confirm the cleaning scope.

What does AC deep cleaning include?

Typical cleaning can include cover removal, filter wash, accessible coil rinse, blower-area cleaning, drain pan cleaning, light odor or visible-buildup treatment, reassembly, and performance testing. Exact scope depends on the unit type and access.

How often should I deep clean my AC?

Many NYC apartments benefit from cleaning before the cooling season. Units in dusty, high-humidity, pet, kitchen, or street-facing spaces may need cleaning more often.

Can you clean visible mold or mildew buildup in my AC?

We can clean visible buildup from accessible parts of the unit and use appropriate cleaner when the equipment design allows. We do not certify mold clearance or perform environmental mold remediation; heavy growth, recurring odor, water damage, or health concerns may require an HVAC or remediation specialist.

What should I send for an AC deep cleaning quote?

Send the unit type, model label, photos of the front cover, filter, and inside condition if accessible, number of units, symptoms such as odor, leaking, or weak airflow, window or sleeve access photos, and parking or building access notes.
