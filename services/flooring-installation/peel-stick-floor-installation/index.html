Common Questions

Peel & Stick FAQ

How much does it cost?

Usually one of the more budget-friendly flooring options, with prep, layout, transitions, and old-floor condition affecting the final quote.

How long does it last?

It depends on product quality, traffic, moisture, and surface prep. We explain when peel-and-stick is practical and when a more durable vinyl product is a better fit.

Is it good for rentals?

It can be, but confirm your lease and product adhesive first. Removal is not always risk-free on every existing floor.

Does the floor need to be smooth?

It works best on a clean, smooth, dry, stable surface. Texture, dust, wax, old adhesive, and moisture can cause lifting.

What should I send for a peel and stick flooring quote?

Send current floor photos, product label or link, room measurements, closeups of surface texture, old adhesive, loose tiles or moisture concerns, transition areas, lease restrictions, whether primer or prep is expected, furniture notes, and building access details.
