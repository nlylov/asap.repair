Want the look of real stone or porcelain tile without the mess? Click-lock tiles snap together without mortar or grout and install in a fraction of the time. Repair Asap LLC installs interlocking tile in kitchens, bathrooms, entryways, and basements across Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, and Staten Island.

Every installation includes subfloor preparation, precise cutting for edges and corners, transition strips, and a full cleanup. Per-square-foot pricing, 1-year workmanship warranty.

We quote flooring, baseboard, and floor repair work across Queens, Manhattan, Brooklyn, the Bronx, Staten Island, and Western Long Island or Nassau County when scope and travel fit. For a faster estimate, text room photos, approximate square footage, flooring material, transition areas, and subfloor or damage photos.

Click-lock flooring installation works well for apartments and rental upgrades when the subfloor is reasonably flat and the client wants a clean floating floor without glue.

We check plank direction, underlayment, expansion gaps, transitions, door clearance, subfloor dips, and whether baseboards or quarter round need to be removed or added.

Send square footage, room photos, flooring box label, and transition areas before booking. Material thickness and subfloor condition drive the final scope.

If the room has heavy furniture, appliances, closets, or uneven thresholds, mention that before booking because moving items and transition work can change the installation plan.

Click-lock flooring rewards careful planning. We dry-fit layout when needed, avoid tiny slivers at walls, stagger seams cleanly, and keep expansion space hidden under trim or transitions so the finished floor can move naturally.

Before installation, we look for high spots, dips, loose existing tiles, swollen laminate, old adhesive ridges, and areas where doors or appliances may rub after the new floor height is added.

Click-lock products are fast only when the room is ready. If subfloor prep, old-floor removal, undercutting door jambs, or adding transitions is needed, we include that in the quote before work begins.

For the fastest answer, text the product link or box label, room measurements, photos of every doorway, and one closeup showing the current floor level at the threshold.

Common Questions

Click-Lock Tile FAQ

What is click-lock tile?

Interlocking tile panels that snap together without mortar. Look like real stone or porcelain.

Where can it be installed?

Kitchens, bathrooms, entryways, laundry rooms, and basements.

How long does it take?

Faster than traditional tile. Standard bathroom or kitchen in about 1 day.

Can it go over existing tile?

Yes, if the surface is flat and level. We assess subfloor before starting.

What should I send for a click-lock flooring quote?

Send the product link or box label, room measurements, photos of every doorway and threshold, current floor or subfloor photos, a closeup showing floor height at the transition, and notes about furniture, old-floor removal, underlayment, transitions, baseboards, and building access.
