What We Repair

Furniture Repairs We Take On

Loose or Wobbly Joints

Chair and table joints that have worked loose are re-glued and clamped.

Legs, Rails & Stretchers

Cracked or broken legs, rails and stretchers on chairs and tables are repaired.

Drawers That Stick or Drop

We work on the runners, slides and glides so drawers open and close properly again.

Sagging Wardrobe & Cabinet Doors

Hinges on wardrobes and freestanding cabinets are re-set or replaced so the doors hang straight. For kitchen cabinet hinges, knobs and pulls, see Cabinet Hardware Installation.

Lifted Edge Veneer

Lifted edge veneer is re-glued.

Failed or Sticky Finish

A finish that stays soft, tacky or cloudy is stripped and refinished, as on the Astoria table.

Metal Frames & Hardware

Stripped or broken bolts, brackets, glides and hardware on metal furniture frames are replaced.

Honest Scope

What We Don't Take On

No Upholstery or Leather Re-Covering

We do not re-cover upholstery, fabric or leather.

No Welding

We do not weld. Metal-frame work is limited to bolts, brackets, glides and hardware.

No Conservation-Grade Antique Restoration

We do not take on antique restoration that requires conservation work.

Inexpensive Flat-Pack: Often Cheaper to Replace

When replacing a low-cost flat-pack piece costs less than repairing it, we will tell you rather than quote the repair.

Common Questions

Wood Furniture Repair FAQ

How much does wood furniture repair cost in NYC?

Most repairs run $165–$450 per item for labor only; materials are extra. Work performed has a $150 minimum. The photo estimate is free, and if an on-site assessment is needed it costs $99 and is credited toward the work.

Is my furniture worth repairing?

Solid wood and mid-range to high-end pieces, in wood or metal, usually are. For inexpensive flat-pack furniture a repair often costs more than replacing the piece; the free photo estimate is the way to check before a visit is booked.

Can a sticky or tacky finish be fixed?

Yes. A sticky or tacky finish is fixed by stripping it and refinishing the piece; another coat on top usually will not fix it. On the Astoria table the soft polyurethane was stripped, the wood sanded, the stain color matched with the client and a new protective polyurethane finish applied. That table took two days to allow for stain drying and curing; timelines vary with the piece and the finish.

Can refinishing be done in my apartment?

Sometimes small touch-ups can be done on site, but refinishing a sticky or failed finish usually needs controlled sanding, stain, ventilation, drying and curing conditions. The Astoria table was handled in a workshop rather than rushed inside the apartment.

Do you do upholstery, welding or antique restoration?

No. We do not re-cover upholstery, fabric or leather, we do not weld, and we do not take on antique restoration that requires conservation work. On metal furniture we replace stripped or broken bolts, brackets, glides and hardware.
