Door repair covers sticking doors, loose hinges, rubbing frames, latch problems, damaged trim, swollen bathroom doors, broken handles, and interior doors that no longer close cleanly.

We check hinge screws, frame alignment, strike plate position, latch depth, seasonal swelling, floor clearance, and whether the issue is the door slab, hardware, or frame movement.

If the door was damaged by a leak, forced entry, pet scratching, or building settling, send photos of both sides and the frame so we can bring the right repair materials.

Door replacement or major frame work may require a separate material pickup or follow-up visit. We flag that before booking instead of treating every door issue as a quick adjustment.

Common Questions

Door Repair FAQ

What problems do you fix?

Sticking, sagging, won't latch, squeaky hinges, damaged frames, holes, and lock issues.

Do you replace doors?

Yes. Pre-hung and slab doors. Measuring, trimming, hinge mortising, and hardware included.

Can you fix a door that won't close?

Yes. Usually shifted hinges, warped frame, or strike plate misalignment.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the repair type, hardware, frame condition, and whether replacement material is needed. Simple adjustments are usually smaller-scope jobs; full replacements are quoted after assessment.

What should I send for a door repair quote?

Send photos of both sides of the door, hinges, latch, strike plate, frame, and the area that rubs or will not close, plus a short note describing sticking, sagging, lock alignment, damage, or whether replacement material may be needed.
