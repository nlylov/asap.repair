Common Questions

Blind Installation FAQ

How much does blind installation cost in NYC?

Pricing depends on window count, blind type, inside or outside mount, frame material, ladder access, bracket hardware, and old-blind removal. Send measurements and product links for a confirmed quote.

Do you install motorized blinds?

Yes. We install battery-powered and compatible smart blinds. Hardwired power or new electrical work needs the proper existing setup or a qualified electrical contractor.

Inside mount or outside mount?

We do both. The right choice depends on window depth, trim, handle clearance, blind size, bracket location, and the look you want.

Can you install blinds in a whole apartment?

Yes. We can often handle full-apartment installations in one visit when measurements, products, access, and hardware are ready.

What should I send for a blind installation quote?

Send the product link, window width and height, inside or outside mount preference, photos of each window frame, wall or frame material if known, number of windows, and any clearance issues around trim, radiators, AC units, or handles.
