Common Questions

Cabinet Hardware Installation FAQ

How much does cabinet hardware installation cost?

We typically charge $5–$10 per piece for knobs/pulls, with a minimum job size. Full-kitchen jobs get volume pricing.

Do you drill new holes?

Yes. We use drilling templates and jigs for precise, consistent hole placement.

Can you install hardware on cabinets without holes?

Yes. We use templates and measuring jigs, then verify spacing and alignment before drilling.

Do you supply the hardware?

We install hardware you provide. We can recommend suppliers if needed.

What should I send for a cabinet hardware installation quote?

Send photos of the cabinet fronts, door and drawer count, hardware product link, pull center-to-center measurement, whether existing holes are present, and any old holes, paint touch-up, or hinge issues that may affect drilling.
