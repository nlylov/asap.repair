Got a broken window or a window that won't open in your NYC apartment? Repair Asap LLC fixes stuck windows, broken glass, failed seals, hardware issues, and drafty weatherstripping across Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, and Staten Island.

We work with many common window types: double-hung, casement, sliding, and awning. Scope may include hardware, balance, latch, screen, trim, weatherstripping, or small seal repairs; glass replacement is reviewed before booking. Scope-based pricing, 1-year workmanship warranty.

We handle handyman repairs and punch-list work across Queens, Manhattan, Brooklyn, the Bronx, Staten Island, and Western Long Island or Nassau County when scope and travel fit. For a faster quote, text clear photos, measurements, product links if parts are involved, and any building access or COI requirements.

Window repair covers stuck sashes, loose handles, damaged screens, drafty trim, minor frame issues, broken latches, and windows that no longer open or close smoothly.

We check the sash, track, balance, latch, weatherstripping, caulk line, and surrounding trim to separate a hardware issue from a frame or moisture problem.

Send closeups of the problem plus one wider photo of the full window. Older NYC windows vary a lot, so photos help identify the right repair approach.

If glass is cracked or a full insulated glass unit must be replaced, we will tell you early whether the work needs a specialty glass vendor instead of a small repair visit.

Window issues can affect comfort, security, and energy use. We focus on practical fixes for operation, latching, drafts, and small damage, and we tell you when replacement or specialty glass work is the more realistic route.

We also test the window several times after adjustment to confirm the repair holds.

We keep the repair practical and explain any limits clearly.

Common Questions

Window Repair FAQ

What do you fix?

Broken glass, stuck windows, locks, latches, cracked frames, weatherstripping, and drafts.

Do you replace glass?

Some single-pane and IGU replacements can be measured and ordered; specialty or large glass may need a glass vendor.

My window won't open?

Usually paint seal, broken balance springs, or track issues. We diagnose and fix it.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on repair type, parts, access, window condition, and whether glass or specialty vendor work is needed.

What should I send for a window repair quote?

Send a full-window photo, closeups of the broken latch, track, sash, glass, seal, or frame issue, the window type if known, whether it opens or locks, access height, and whether glass replacement or specialty vendor work may be needed.
