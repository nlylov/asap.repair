Common Questions

Faucet FAQ

What faucets do you install?

Kitchen, bathroom, bathtub, laundry, and bar faucets are reviewed against the existing sink, hole count, shut-offs, and supply lines.

Do you supply the faucet?

You provide it or we source it. We confirm product and cost first.

What should I send for a fast quote?

Send the faucet product link or model number, photos above and under the sink, the number of sink holes, shut-off valve photos, drain or pop-up details, countertop material, and any building or COI requirements.

How long does it take?

Standard swaps often take 1-2 hours. Corroded shut-offs, tight cabinets, old supply lines, or drain alignment changes can add time.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on fixture type, access, shut-off condition, supply line condition, drain alignment, and parts needed.
