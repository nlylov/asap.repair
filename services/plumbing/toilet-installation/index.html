Common Questions

Toilet FAQ

What types do you install?

Standard round, elongated, comfort-height, dual-flush, skirted, smart-toilet, and bidet-seat setups are reviewed for rough-in distance, flange condition, floor level, shut-off access, and building rules. Wall-hung carrier or rough-in changes require separate scope review.

Do you remove the old toilet?

We can disconnect and remove the existing toilet when the scope is approved. Haul-away depends on building rules and the quote.

Can you fix a running toilet?

Yes. Fill valve, flapper, flush valve, handle, tank gasket, supply-line, and bidet-seat issues are reviewed before recommending repair or replacement.

What should I send for a fast quote?

Send a photo of the existing toilet, the new toilet model link, shut-off valve photo, floor and flange area photo if visible, rough-in distance if known, disposal needs, and any building or COI requirements.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on toilet type, flange and floor condition, shut-off valve condition, supply line needs, haul-away, and building access.
