Handyman Service FAQ

How much do handyman services cost in NYC?

Pricing depends on scope, access, materials, building rules, urgency, and photos or product details. Work performed starts at our $150 minimum. The $99 on-site assessment is a separate visit — we come out, diagnose and document — and it is credited toward the job if you go ahead, so it costs nothing when we do the work. Photo and text estimates are always free, and we confirm a transparent quote before work begins.

What should I send for a faster quote?

Send photos, product links or model numbers, measurements, service address or neighborhood, preferred timing, parking or access details, and any building or COI requirements.

Do you serve all five NYC boroughs?

Yes. Repair Asap LLC serves Queens, Manhattan, Brooklyn, the Bronx, and Staten Island, plus Western Long Island or Nassau County when scope and travel fit.

Which services are best for apartment move-ins?

Common move-in requests include furniture assembly, TV mounting, shelf and curtain rod installation, mirror or art hanging, smart lock or thermostat setup, blind installation, minor repairs, and new apartment setup.

Do you provide COI for apartment buildings?

Yes. When a building requires a certificate of insurance, send the building's COI sample or requirements, management contact details, service address, requested wording, and the planned service scope before the appointment is confirmed.

Not Sure Which Service You Need?

Send photos and a short description. Repair ASAP will confirm the right category and quote the work before scheduling.
