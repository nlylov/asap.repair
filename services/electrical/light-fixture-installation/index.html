Common Questions

Light Fixture FAQ

How much does installation cost?

Pricing depends on fixture type, ceiling height, fixture weight, box condition, dimmer compatibility, and access. Simple swaps cost less than heavy chandeliers or recessed-style layout work.

Can you add a fixture where there isn't one?

New fixture locations may require a new box, electrical routing, a NYC Licensed Master Electrician, and DOB permit. Send photos first so we can confirm whether the request fits replacement scope.

Do you install heavy chandeliers?

We review fixture weight, ceiling box rating, ceiling height, mounting plate, and support needs before booking. Heavy fixtures may require additional support or Licensed Master Electrician scope.

What should I send for a fast quote?

Send the fixture product link, fixture weight if listed, photos of the current fixture and ceiling box or canopy, a wider room photo, ceiling height, switch or dimmer details, and any building or COI requirements.

Can you add recessed lighting?

Recessed lighting and new fixture layouts often involve ceiling openings, added electrical routes, and permit-level electrical scope. We review photos and product details first before scheduling.
