Common Questions

Chandelier Installation FAQ

How much does chandelier installation cost in NYC?

Chandelier installation is quoted after fixture weight, ceiling height, box rating, mounting plate, assembly time, access, and support needs are reviewed.

Can you install a heavy chandelier?

We review heavy fixtures case by case. The existing ceiling box and support must be rated for the fixture, and additional support or Licensed Master Electrician scope may be required.

Do you remove old fixtures?

We can remove an accessible old fixture as part of a replacement when the existing box, wiring, and building rules fit the job.

What should I send for a fast quote?

Send the chandelier product link, fixture weight, ceiling height, photos of the current fixture and ceiling canopy, room photo, dimmer or switch details, crystal or shade assembly needs, and any building or COI requirements.

Can you install on a high ceiling?

High-ceiling chandelier work is reviewed for ceiling height, ladder access, fixture weight, and whether two-person handling is needed before booking.
