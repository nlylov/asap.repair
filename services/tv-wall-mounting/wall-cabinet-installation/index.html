Common Questions

Wall Cabinet Installation FAQ

How much does wall cabinet installation cost?

Pricing depends on cabinet count, size, weight, wall material, stud layout, rail or direct-fastening hardware, door swing, clearance, contents, and access.

Do you install IKEA wall cabinets?

Yes, when the cabinet system, rail hardware, wall type, and load are appropriate. Send the IKEA model or product link and wall photos before booking.

How do you secure cabinets to the wall?

We locate studs and review backing, cabinet weight, hardware, and expected contents. Depending on the system, mounting may use rails, lag bolts, blocking, masonry anchors, or other load-appropriate fasteners.

What should I send for a fast quote?

Send cabinet product links or model numbers, cabinet count, dimensions and weight, wall photos, desired placement, expected contents, rail or hardware details, appliance or hood clearances, and any building or COI requirements.

Can you install a row of kitchen cabinets?

Often yes, after reviewing wall straightness, stud layout, cabinet run, rail system, level lines, appliance or hood clearance, and whether two installers are needed.
