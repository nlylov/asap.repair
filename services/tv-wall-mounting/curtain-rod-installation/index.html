Common Questions

Curtain Rod FAQ

How much does curtain rod installation cost in NYC?

Pricing depends on window count, rod length, wall or ceiling material, bracket spacing, drape weight, ladder access, and whether the scope includes double rods, tracks, bay windows, or motorized systems. Use our instant calculator for a starting estimate.

Can you install curtain rods on concrete or plaster walls?

Often yes. Concrete, brick, plaster, tile, and metal-stud walls may need different anchors or may limit placement, so we review the wall, rod system, drape weight, and drilling access first.

Should curtain rods be mounted into studs?

Studs are preferred for heavy drapes when placement allows. If studs are not where brackets need to go, we review wall condition and hardware options before confirming the load and layout.

Do you install ceiling-mounted curtain tracks?

Often yes. Ceiling tracks depend on ceiling material, joists or anchors, track type, drape weight, ladder access, and whether power or motorized controls are involved.
