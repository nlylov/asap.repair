Common Questions

TV Mounting FAQ

How much does TV mounting cost in NYC?

Pricing depends on TV size and weight, wall type, mount style, cable path, outlet location, soundbar scope, parking, and building access. Use our instant calculator for a starting estimate, then send photos so we can confirm the scope.

Can you mount a TV on a brick or concrete wall?

Often yes. Drywall, plaster, brick, concrete, tile, stone, and metal studs each need a different mounting method, so we review the wall, TV weight, bracket, and drilling access before confirming the job.

Do you hide the cables?

We can use surface raceways and some low-voltage in-wall kits where the wall and building rules allow it. New outlets, power relocation, and other electrical work are quoted separately.

What should I send for a fast quote?

Send the TV size or model, mount type or product link, wall photo, desired height, wall material if known, outlet and cable location, soundbar details, fireplace or tile conditions, and any building or COI requirements.

Can you mount a TV above a fireplace?

Sometimes. Fireplace mounting depends on heat clearance, viewing height, wall structure, outlet location, cable path, and whether drilling into masonry, tile, or stone is appropriate.
