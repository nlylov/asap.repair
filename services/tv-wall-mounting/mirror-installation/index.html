Common Questions

Mirror Mounting FAQ

How do you mount a heavy mirror on drywall?

We look for studs first, then choose mirror clips, cleats, toggles, rails, or other hardware based on mirror weight, backing, wall condition, and the manufacturer's mounting points.

Can you mount a frameless mirror in a bathroom?

Often yes. We review mirror size, backing, tile or drywall condition, moisture exposure, hardware, and whether adhesive or clips are appropriate before confirming the scope.

How much does mirror mounting cost in NYC?

Pricing depends on mirror size, weight, wall type, mounting method, height, access, and whether tile or masonry drilling is involved. Use our instant calculator for a starting estimate, then send photos for a confirmed quote.

Can you mount a mirror on a brick or tile wall?

Often yes. Tile, stone, brick, and concrete require specific bits, anchors, and drilling access, and some surfaces may limit the mounting method after review.
