Common Questions

Shelf Mounting FAQ

How much does shelf mounting cost in NYC?

Pricing depends on the number of shelves, shelf type, bracket design, wall material, expected load, spacing, and access. Use our instant calculator for a starting estimate, then send shelf and wall photos for a confirmed quote.

Can you mount shelves on brick or concrete walls?

Often yes. Brick, concrete, plaster, tile, and metal-stud walls require different anchors and can limit the load, so we review the wall, shelf system, and drilling access before booking.

How much weight can a floating shelf hold?

It depends on shelf design, bracket spacing, mounting hardware, wall condition, and what you plan to store. We use the product rating and wall review to choose the strongest practical mounting method.

What should I send for a fast quote?

Send shelf product links or photos, shelf dimensions, number of shelves, expected load, bracket or hardware details, wall photos, desired spacing and height, and any building or COI requirements.

Do you supply the shelves or just install them?

We usually install shelves you've already purchased. If you need help choosing, send the wall photo, desired size, and load expectation first so we can suggest a shelf and hardware style that fits the wall.
