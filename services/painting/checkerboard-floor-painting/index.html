Repair Asap LLC offers professional checkerboard floor painting across all five boroughs of New York City. The painted checkerboard floor is a timeless design element — seen in classic NYC brownstones, pre-war apartments, and modern lofts alike. Whether you want a crisp black-and-white diamond pattern for your entryway or a custom two-tone color scheme for your kitchen, our painters deliver precision geometry with lasting durability.

Every project starts with surface review, sanding or filling where needed, priming, measured pattern layout, paint-system planning, and a protective topcoat when the floor and use case fit. We work on hardwood, plywood, concrete, and previously painted surfaces when the existing condition can support a painted finish.

We handle interior painting, wall repair, wallpaper, cabinet painting, and decorative finishes across Queens, Manhattan, Brooklyn, the Bronx, Staten Island, and Western Long Island or Nassau County when scope and travel fit. For a faster quote, text photos of each wall or surface, dimensions, finish inspiration, paint or material details, and any building access requirements.

Painted floors are more sensitive than tile or vinyl. Existing finish, moisture, floor movement, pet traffic, chair legs, cleaning products, and the topcoat system all affect durability.

For a useful estimate, send straight-on floor photos, closeups of scratches or peeling, room dimensions, the subfloor or existing finish type, desired square size, and color references.

The Process

How It Works

01

Measure & Plan

We review the floor, measure the room, discuss colors and tile sizes, and confirm whether the surface is a good candidate before finalizing the scope.

02

Prep, Tape & Paint

We sand, prime, and prepare the surface. Then we measure and tape a precise grid, paint the base color, and apply the contrasting squares by hand.

03

Seal & Protect

After the paint dries, we apply the approved protective topcoat system and explain cure time, furniture precautions, and maintenance expectations.
