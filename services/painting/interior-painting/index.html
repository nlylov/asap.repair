Common Questions

Interior Painting FAQ

How much does it cost?

Per-room pricing. Includes prep, 2 coats, ceiling, trim, cleanup. Quoted after measuring.

Do you move furniture?

Yes. We move, cover, and protect everything. Floors get drop cloths.

What paint do you use?

Benjamin Moore, Sherwin-Williams, or your preferred brand. Low-VOC, premium-grade.

How long does it take?

Standard room: 1 day. Full apartment: 2–4 days.

What should I send for an interior painting quote?

Send wide photos of each room, closeups of damaged walls or stains, approximate room dimensions, ceiling height, whether ceilings or trim are included, paint colors or finish preferences, furniture moving needs, and building access rules.
