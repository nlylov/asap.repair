Repair Asap LLC offers a professional interior painting service in New York City for apartments, condos, co-ops, and rental units. Whether you're refreshing a single bedroom, repainting an entire apartment before move-in, or repairing damaged drywall before a lease ends, our experienced technicians deliver clean, even coverage with meticulous prep and zero mess. Our interior painting service NYC clients trust covers walls, ceilings, trim, doors, and cabinets.

We regularly paint apartments in Queens — Forest Hills, Rego Park, Astoria, Jackson Heights, Flushing — and Brooklyn (Williamsburg, Park Slope, Bay Ridge, Bushwick, Flatbush, Bed-Stuy). Manhattan clients from the Upper East Side to Hell's Kitchen and Chelsea book us for pre-move-in repaints and accent walls. Landlords and property managers rely on us for fast, clean turnover painting between tenants.

Every painting quote reviews surface prep: filling nail holes, sanding, taping edges, and protecting floors with drop cloths. We usually plan two finish coats over primer when the surface and color change call for it. Drywall repair, skim coating, or wallpaper removal before painting is scoped separately when the wall needs more than normal prep.

We handle interior painting, wall repair, wallpaper, cabinet painting, and decorative finishes across Queens, Manhattan, Brooklyn, the Bronx, Staten Island, and Western Long Island or Nassau County when scope and travel fit. For a faster quote, text photos of each wall or surface, dimensions, finish inspiration, paint or material details, and any building access requirements.

For the most accurate painting estimate, send wide photos of each room, closeups of damage or peeling paint, approximate dimensions, ceiling height, whether ceilings or trim are included, and the paint color or finish you want.

We separate cosmetic painting from wall repair. Small nail holes and minor scuffs are normal prep; water stains, loose plaster, failed skim coat, active moisture, or heavy wallpaper adhesive may need repair before finish paint.

Related project context: our Manhattan barbershop renovation shows painting as part of a full commercial finish sequence, and the Brooklyn drywall repair shows patch, compound, dry, prep, and paint work after wall damage.
