# Appliance Repair and Commercial Refrigeration Expansion Plan

Date: 2026-07-09
Site: https://asap.repair/
Market: New York City, five boroughs, Western Long Island, Nassau County when route and scope fit

## Executive decision

Repair Asap is a strong brand fit for appliance repair, appliance installation, AC installation/cleaning, and commercial refrigeration triage because the name is broad, urgent, and service-oriented. It does not lock the company into "handyman only." The risk is not the name; the risk is operational overpromising. Public pages must separate:

- In-scope handyman-compatible appliance work: visible diagnostics, cleaning, drain/vent/hookup issues, leveling, replacement planning, installation, photo-based triage.
- Regulated or specialist work: refrigerant, sealed-system refrigerator work, compressor replacement, gas piping, new circuits, hidden plumbing, DOB permit scope, warranty-authorized manufacturer repair.

The site should expand the appliance category in phases, starting with high-intent residential appliance pages and a cautious commercial refrigeration triage page. Full commercial refrigeration repair should only be published once EPA Section 608 coverage and parts/vendor workflow are confirmed.

## External constraints that shape the plan

- EPA Section 608: technicians who maintain, service, repair, or dispose of equipment that could release regulated refrigerants must be certified. EPA says Section 608 credentials do not expire. Source: https://www.epa.gov/section608/section-608-technician-certification
- EPA refrigerant sales restriction: only EPA-certified technicians can purchase refrigerants for stationary refrigeration and air-conditioning equipment, with limited exceptions. Source: https://www.epa.gov/section608/refrigerant-sales-restriction
- NYC home improvement contractor license: NYC says a person or business must have a Home Improvement Contractor license for construction, repair, remodeling, or other home improvement work to residential land or buildings. Source: https://www.nyc.gov/site/dca/businesses/license-checklist-home-improvement-contractor.page
- National appliance repair pricing context: published consumer cost guides show refrigerator, dishwasher, washer, dryer, oven/range, microwave, freezer, and disposal repairs as common paid categories, with service calls and emergency calls monetizable. Source: https://www.thespruce.com/appliance-repair-cost-8386853

## Growth assessment

Residential appliance repair is a strong near-term growth path because it is already adjacent to existing installation pages, matches current customer behavior, and produces practical repair-or-replace leads. It should increase organic coverage for "refrigerator repair NYC", "dishwasher repair NYC", "washer repair NYC", and "dryer repair NYC" without changing the company identity.

Commercial refrigeration has higher ticket size and urgency, especially for restaurants, delis, cafes, groceries, offices, and property managers. It can become a strong money category, but only if the compliance and fulfillment path is real. The first public version should be conservative: diagnostics, cleaning, airflow, temperature confirmation, maintenance planning, and referral/partner routing for refrigerant or sealed-system work.

The Repair Asap name fits both residential and commercial appliance work. It suggests fast repair, not only handyman tasks. For franchise or national expansion, it is broad enough to support appliance repair, commercial maintenance, turnover services, and installation categories. The name should be supported by category-specific page titles, not changed.
