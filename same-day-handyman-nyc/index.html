Safety First

What Is Not a Same-Day Handyman Visit

Some situations should never wait for a handyman, ours or anyone else's. Handle the emergency first; we can help with the cleanup and repairs afterward.

Active gas smell

Leave the apartment, do not flip switches or light anything, and call 911 and Con Edison from outside. Gas piping, valves and leaks are never handyman work. Book us later for anything the incident left behind.

Burst pipe or active flooding

Close the fixture shutoff, or have the super close the building shutoff, and call a licensed emergency plumber. Once the water is stopped, we can quote the drywall, flooring and fixture work that follows.

Electrical burning smell or hot outlet

Turn the circuit off at the breaker if it is safe to reach, and call a Licensed Master Electrician, or 911 if there is smoke. Wiring faults are flagged and routed, not patched; we handle fixture and device work after the circuit is cleared.

Refrigerant, compressor or sealed-system failure

This may require an EPA Section 608 certified technician, the manufacturer or a specialist partner. We flag that before scheduling, and can quote replacement planning and installation instead if repair is not worth it.

What to Send for a Same-Day Shot

Every item below removes a reason to postpone. A complete first message is the single biggest factor in landing today's slot.

Photos of the problem

Wide shot for context, close-up for detail. For appliances, include the surrounding access area, rear or under-unit if reachable.

Brand and model number

The sticker is usually inside the door frame, behind the kickplate or on the back. It decides parts, tools and whether the job fits scope at all.

+

Exact symptom and when it started

What changed, when, any error code, and a short video if there is a noise or a drip. Guesswork is what slow visits are made of.

+

Address or neighborhood

Same-day depends on where today's route already is. A borough and cross-street is enough to check the fit.

+

Access and building rules

Doorman, freight elevator hours, COI requirements, alteration desk, pets, parking. Every rule discovered on arrival costs the slot.

+

Time windows you can host today

Two or three workable windows beat one narrow one. The wider your availability, the better the odds an opening lines up.

Honest Answers

Same-Day Handyman FAQ

Are you a 24/7 emergency handyman?

No. Repair Asap LLC works scheduled visits during business hours, and the fastest response happens during business hours. You can send photos and details any time, and we reply as soon as we are open. Active gas smells, flooding and electrical burning smells belong with 911, Con Edison, a licensed emergency plumber or a Licensed Master Electrician, not a handyman.

Do you guarantee same-day service?

No, and we will not pretend otherwise. Same-day visits happen when a slot is open on the calendar and the scope fits. Sending photos, the model number and your access details upfront makes a same-day fit much more likely, because we can confirm scope and quote without a first trip. If today is full, we offer the next open slot.

Which boroughs do you cover for same-day requests?

Queens, Manhattan, Brooklyn, the Bronx and Staten Island, plus Western Long Island and Nassau County when scope and travel fit. Same-day fit depends on where the day's route already is, so include your neighborhood in the first message.

Can you handle building COI requirements on short notice?

We support Certificates of Insurance for managed buildings, condos and co-ops. Send the building's COI sample or requirements with your first message; paperwork approval time is set by your management office, and a same-day visit is only possible if the building can process it that fast.

Why do you ask for photos and a model number first?

Photos and the model number let us confirm scope fit, quote fast and arrive with the right parts and tools, which is what makes a single same-day visit realistic. They also let us flag work that needs a Licensed Master Plumber, a Licensed Master Electrician, an EPA Section 608 certified technician or the manufacturer before anyone books a wasted appointment.

What happens if you cannot fit me in today?

We say so directly and offer the next open slot, usually next-day. Where it helps, we suggest safe interim steps, like shutting a supply valve, moving food to a working fridge or keeping a door secured. If the issue needs a specialist, we flag that immediately so you are not waiting on the wrong trade.
