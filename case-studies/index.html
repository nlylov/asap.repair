Door Replacement📍 Staten Island

Entry Door and Frame Replacement in Staten Island

A tired rear entry had an old aluminum storm door and a glass-insert door hung in a worn frame. Repair Asap removed both doors and the frame, set a new prehung entry door plumb and level, installed a single lock, capped the unused second lock hole, and sealed the perimeter with spray foam in about three and a half hours. A new storm door was installed over it afterward, with its own lock and an automatic closer.
